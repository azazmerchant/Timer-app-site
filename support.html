SUBSCRIPTIONS

  • Cancel: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Focbit. You keep Premium until the end of the time you paid for.
  • Restore a purchase: in the app, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase belongs to your Apple ID.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all App Store payments. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How does a focus round work?

Pick your hero, choose how long to focus and the round starts. Your hero walks into their room and works with you until the timer ends. Finish the round to open your reward chest.

Why can't I pause the timer?

Pausing makes it too easy to drift off. Once a round starts, the only way out is to hold GIVE UP for five seconds, so you always have a moment to change your mind.

What happens if I give up?

The round ends and doesn't count. You keep everything you have already earned, and you can start a new round right away.

How do I change my hero?

On your dashboard, tap CHANGE HERO. Your progress stays with you, and each hero keeps their own count of rounds.

Is it free?

Your first 2 focus rounds are free with any hero. After that, Premium unlocks unlimited rounds: monthly, yearly, or one payment for life.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Where do the numbers come from?

From published research we found online, like average reading speed or calories burned. They are estimates. The app's About page lists every source.

How do I turn the sounds off?

Tap the speaker in the top-right corner of any screen. Sounds also stay quiet when your iPhone is on silent.

Does the app block other apps?

Not yet. The apps you pick are your plan for what to stay away from. Real blocking with Apple's Screen Time is coming in an update.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 24 October 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Focbit: Focus Timer does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your iPhone.

This policy explains how the Focbit: Focus Timer app for iPhone ("Focbit", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. If you have a question, email us at focbittimer@gmail.com.

WHAT THE APP SAVES ON YOUR IPHONE

To work, the app saves a few things in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your settings: whether you finished the intro, the apps you chose to stay away from, the hero you play as, and whether sounds are on.
  • Your progress: focus rounds finished, minutes focused, XP, level, day streak, and minutes per day for the last 60 days.
  • Your free rounds used and which Premium plan you have, if any.
  • If you cancel Premium, the reason you pick and any note you write.

This information never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Like any app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your iPhone settings.

WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising ID, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.

PURCHASES

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the app whether you have an active plan. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

WHEN YOU EMAIL US

If you use Contact Us in the app, your iPhone's Mail app opens with a message to us. The app adds its version number and your iOS version to the message to help us fix problems. When you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply to you, we don't share it, and we delete it if you ask.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

THIS WEBSITE

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when you visit. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the app ever starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

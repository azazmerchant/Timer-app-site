FOCUS TIMER

Put the phone down and pick a hero. They walk into their own pixel room and get to work with you until the timer ends.

HOW IT WORKS

PICK A ROUND

Choose 15, 25 or 50 minutes, or any time you like. Your hero starts working the moment you do.

NO EASY WAY OUT

There's no pause button. To quit you have to hold GIVE UP for five seconds, so you always get a moment to change your mind.

EARN YOUR CHEST

Finish a round to burst open a reward chest, level up, and see what your focus was really worth.

MEET THE HEROES

Seven heroes: Student, Coder, Fitness, Trader, Creator, Astronomer and Thinker. Each one has a him and a her, and a room of their own.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your rounds, streak and XP are saved on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Questions, bugs or ideas, we read every message.
